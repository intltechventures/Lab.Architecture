
# US Privacy Regulation: Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA)

# Summary 

- The Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) is a U.S. federal law enacted in 1998 and effective April 21, 2000. It protects the privacy of children under 13. The law requires website and online service operators to post clear privacy policies, get verifiable parental consent before collecting personal data, and keep children's data secure.
